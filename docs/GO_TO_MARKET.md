# Go-to-Market

Last updated 2026-11-14. Evidence: `MARKET_RESEARCH.md`, `COMPETITIVE_ANALYSIS.md`.

## 1. Positioning

**For** acquisition teams that pay for inbound and consented seller leads, **Northstar** is the AI acquisitions desk that answers every lead in under a minute, qualifies it, underwrites it, and books the appointment — inside the CRM you already use — **unlike** all-in-one investor CRMs that require migration and treat underwriting and compliance as afterthoughts.

Message hierarchy:
1. *Never lose a paid lead to slow response.* (speed to lead)
2. *Know your number before you call.* (underwriting with transparent math)
3. *Compliance you can prove.* (consent, opt-outs, quiet hours, audit trail)
4. *Keep your CRM.* (write-back, no migration)

## 2. Motion: founder-led sales → design partners → case studies

| Phase | Goal | Actions | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Discovery (weeks 0–4) | Validate pains and willingness to pay | 30 ICP interviews (BiggerPockets, REI meetups, GHL/Podio communities, PPC lead-vendor customers); Van Westendorp survey | ≥ 15 teams confirm speed-to-lead + underwriting pain; price band confirmed |
| 1. Design partners (weeks 4–16) | 10 teams live | 90-day pilot at 50 % list; weekly reviews; instrument baseline vs. Northstar | Measured: first-response time, qualification completeness, cost per appointment |
| 2. Case studies (weeks 12–24) | 3 publishable outcomes | Before/after metrics, AM testimonials, compliance evidence | ≥ 5 paid conversions |
| 3. Repeatable sales (months 6–12) | Predictable pipeline | Content (underwriting methodology, compliance guides), webinars with lead vendors, GHL Marketplace listing, partner referral fees | CAC payback < 6 months |

## 3. Channels (ranked)

1. **Founder-led outbound** to teams buying PPC/SEO leads (identifiable via Carrot sites, ads).
2. **Partnerships:** PPC lead vendors, Carrot-site agencies, GHL agencies (reseller margin), dispositions platforms.
3. **Community/content:** transparent underwriting methodology, state compliance explainers (counsel-reviewed).
4. **Marketplace:** GoHighLevel app marketplace.

Avoid: paid acquisition before case studies; selling AI cold-calling.

## 4. Pilot success metrics (shared with each partner)

- Median time to first response (target < 60 s inside contact window).
- % leads with complete qualification + draft MAO before AM's first call (target ≥ 70 %).
- Cost per qualified appointment vs. baseline (target −30 %).
- Compliance incidents (target 0); opt-out latency (target < 60 s).
